# 66MM Preview — 26 October 2026 Refinement

These are owner-requested UI preview rules. All records remain fictional and session-local. Production implementation and its verification remain separate work.

- Each operational shift starts at 18:00 and ends at 12:00 the following day, Asia/Kuala_Lumpur. Starts from 00:00 to before 12:00 belong to the prior shift start day; 12:00–17:59 is outside the defined shift and is explicitly labelled, without inventing an assignment to a shift. Actual timestamps remain visible. Work-day filters, financial month grouping, current shift earnings and financial transaction details use the originating shift day. Cross-month early-morning events retain the prior month's shift attribution. Calendar leave dates are unchanged.
- Short interface labels use title case, with small connecting words kept lowercase; sentences retain sentence case. Acronyms such as QR, ID and MYR are preserved. Chinese text remains uncased. This helper also applies to subsequent copy loaded through the common label resources.
- Bottom navigation includes a centered role/venue/destination breadcrumb.
- Customer bulk selection starts empty and appears as a minimized overlay after the first explicit bulk addition. Individual × controls and Remove All deselect staff. Profile navigation and outside clicks minimize it. Staff has a read-only Roster with no bulk controls/sidebar.
- Customer Active booking cards include their full deduplicated information and direct Change/Cancellation and Manager Help actions. Legacy customer detail links redirect to the Active list; there is no extra customer detail screen. Existing contact gates and request-review behavior remain.
- Customer venue and Staff navigation now use Roster. Current unavailability desaturates the portrait and adds an indicator. Availability appears between Public Profile and Languages Spoken, with the 18:00–12:00 shift timeline. Off/medical leave disables the timeline and says Unavailable for the Day. Open-ended current service does not promise a release time; package shift locks remain represented.
- Profile photo thumbnails update the large inline image. Staff lightbox/enlarge controls are removed; the separate room gallery remains. Customer actions are Reserve Staff and Add to Bulk Selection. These presentation indicators do not silently establish real inventory or successful reservations.
- Manager Floor and Bookings use the available viewport height, with scrolling inside the list and no page scrolling. Compact styles apply to shorter viewports.
- Manager Bookings has Current / Past Bookings and consistent status filters. Only Active, Scheduled, Late, Conflict and Completed tags are used. The owner explicitly chose to keep pending requests in Actions. Cancelled/rejected outcomes retain their existing Actions/customer history; they are not mislabeled Completed.
- Confirmed bounded overlaps or simultaneous active assignments can show Conflict; an unknown ordinary service end is not fabricated to infer an overlap. No automatic release, cancellation or billing change occurs.
- Thick booking rows display status, type, customer, place, assigned names, actual start/end and applicable duration/price. Names are used instead of tiny portraits. The centered, scrollable popup sits above the footer and retains customer/contact/profile, service, package controls and completed-spend components. Other unrelated packages are not included.
- Completed records stay in Current for less than two hours after their recorded completion, then become visible in Past Bookings by shift date. A refresh timer updates this projection; durable retention is not simulated. Past date selection allows the current period plus three prior finalized demo months. Completion does not delete source records.
